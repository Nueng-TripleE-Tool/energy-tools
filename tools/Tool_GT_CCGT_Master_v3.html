💨 Multi-Pressure Steam Turbine Work (Σ enthalpy)

    
W_ST = Σ [ṁᵢ·(h_in,i − h_out,i)]·η_mech | แต่ละ section: h_out = h_in − η_isen·(h_in − h_out,s) | η_ST,isen = (h_in − h_out)/(h_in − h_out,s)
